Aviation Maintenance Project: Main Gear System Replacement

Replacing the left and right main landing gear legs on a Cirrus SR22 GTS X was a project that required careful planning, precision, and strict adherence to maintenance procedures. The aircraft was brought in for maintenance after wear and inspection findings indicated that both main gear legs required replacement to maintain safe operating conditions.

The process began with securing and properly supporting the aircraft before removing the existing landing gear assemblies. Detailed inspections were performed on the surrounding attachment points, hardware, and structural components to ensure there was no additional damage or corrosion that could affect the installation. Once the old gear legs were removed, the new assemblies were carefully fitted and installed according to manufacturer specifications.

Throughout the project, special attention was given to alignment, torque requirements, and system checks to ensure the landing gear operated as intended. Following installation, comprehensive inspections and functional tests were completed to verify the integrity of the repair and confirm the aircraft met all applicable airworthiness standards.v

This project highlighted the importance of precision workmanship and attention to detail in aircraft maintenance. Successfully completing the replacement restored the aircraft's landing gear system to optimal condition, ensuring continued safety, reliability, and performance for future operations.
