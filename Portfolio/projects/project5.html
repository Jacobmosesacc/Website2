CAD Design & Engineering Portfolio

The CAD design and engineering portfolio represents a structured collection of projects focused on translating engineering concepts into precise digital models. Each design was developed with attention to accuracy, functionality, manufacturability, and real-world application.

The process typically began with understanding the design requirements and constraints of each component or system. From there, conceptual sketches were translated into detailed 3D models using CAD software, ensuring correct dimensions, tolerances, and material considerations were reflected in the design.

A strong emphasis was placed on engineering integrity and practical usability. Components were not only modeled for visual representation but also evaluated for structural performance, assembly compatibility, and operational efficiency. Where necessary, designs were iterated to improve strength, reduce weight, or simplify manufacturing processes.

Assembly modeling played a key role in validating how individual parts interact within a system. This helped identify potential interferences, alignment issues, and areas for optimization before physical production.

Throughout the portfolio, the focus remained on producing clean, production-ready models that could transition from digital environments to real-world manufacturing with minimal revision.

This body of work demonstrates a strong foundation in CAD principles, engineering problem-solving, and design thinking applied to practical mechanical systems.
