Restoring a Carbon Fiber Wing Fairing

The repair of a damaged carbon fiber wing fairing required a structured and highly controlled composite maintenance process to restore both structural integrity and aerodynamic performance. The component had sustained impact damage during ground operations, resulting in visible surface cracking and suspected subsurface delamination.

The process began with a detailed inspection to assess the full extent of the damage. Non-destructive evaluation techniques, including tap testing, were used to map out hidden delamination beyond the visible impact area. Once the damage boundaries were confirmed, the affected section was carefully marked for removal.

Controlled material removal was carried out using approved composite repair methods to eliminate damaged fibers while preserving the surrounding structure. The repair area was then prepared with precise surface profiling to ensure proper bonding conditions for the new laminate.

After curing, the repair area was inspected for bond quality, voids, and surface finish. Final blending and finishing restored the aerodynamic profile of the wing fairing, ensuring minimal airflow disruption.

The successful completion of this repair returned the component to serviceable condition, maintaining both safety and performance standards required in modern composite aircraft structures.
